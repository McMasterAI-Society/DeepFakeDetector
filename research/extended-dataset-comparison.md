# Dataset Research Summary

| **Name** | **Size** | **Real : AI-Generated** | **AI Generators Represented** | **Availability** | **Licensing** |
|-----------|-----------|--------------------------|--------------------------------|------------------|---------------|
| **[GenImage (NeurIPS DB 2023)](https://github.com/gen-image/GenImage)** | ~2.68M images | 1,350,000 : 1,331,167 | Diffusion + GANs (SD, ADM, GLIDE, BigGAN, VQDM; ImageNet-class coverage) | [GitHub – GenImage](https://github.com/gen-image/GenImage) | Research dataset (see repo license) |
| **[WildFake (2024)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.11635)** | 3.57M images | 1,013,446 : 2,557,278 | GANs, Diffusion (SD 1.x/2.x/XL, Midjourney v5, ADM), Others | [Hugging Face – WildFake Dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/IIPL/WildFake) | Research use (check mirror terms) |
| **[ArtiFact (ICIP 2023)](https://github.com/grip-unina/ArtiFact)** | 2,496,738 images | 964,989 : 1,531,749 | 25 generator families (13 GANs, 7 Diffusion, 5 Misc) + 8 real sources | [Kaggle – ArtiFact Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/francescomerra/artifact-ai-generated-images) | Mixed (many non-commercial; see dataset table) |
| **[Community Forensics (CVPR 2025)](https://huggingface.co/datasets/forensicsai/community-forensics)** | 2.7M generated | 0 : 2,700,000 | 4,803 models (Stable Diffusion variants, commercial and open) | [Hugging Face – Community Forensics](https://huggingface.co/datasets/forensicsai/community-forensics) | CC BY-4.0 + inherits model licenses |
| **[DiffusionDB (2022)](https://huggingface.co/datasets/poloclub/diffusiondb)** | 14M generated (+ 2M subset) | 0 : 14,000,000 | Stable Diffusion (prompt + params from users) | [Hugging Face – DiffusionDB](https://huggingface.co/datasets/poloclub/diffusiondb) | CC0-1.0 (Public Domain) |
| **[Diffusion Forensics / DIRE (ICCV 2023)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.04275)** | 60k – 531k | ~92k : 439k | 8 – 11 diffusion models (LSUN, ImageNet, CelebA-HQ) | [Paper – Diffusion Forensics Benchmark](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.04275) | Research use only |
| **[Synthbuster (2024)](https://zenodo.org/records/10473476)** | 9,000 images | 0 : 9,000 | DALL·E 2/3, Adobe Firefly, Midjourney v5, SD 1.3/1.4/2/XL, GLIDE | [Zenodo – Synthbuster Dataset](https://zenodo.org/records/10473476) | Research dataset |
| **[CiFAKE (2022)](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2352340923001187)** | 120,000 images | 60,000 : 60,000 | Stable Diffusion v1.4 (CIFAR-10 scale) | [Kaggle – CiFAKE Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/kritanjalijain/cifake-real-and-ai-generated-synthetic-images) | Research only |
| **[FaceForensics++ (2019)](https://github.com/ondyari/FaceForensics)** | 1,000 original videos + 4 manipulation types | ~1,000 : 4,000 videos (millions of frames) | Deepfakes, Face2Face, FaceSwap, NeuralTextures | [Project Page – FaceForensics++](https://github.com/ondyari/FaceForensics) | Research terms of use |
| **[Celeb-DF v2 (2020)](https://github.com/yuezunli/celeb-deepfakeforensics)** | 6,229 videos | 590 : 5,639 | Classic DeepFake (Autoencoder FaceSwap) | [GitHub – Celeb-DF v2](https://github.com/yuezunli/celeb-deepfakeforensics) | Research terms of use |
